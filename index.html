Patient & target

KDIGO 2012 recommends delivering 20–25 mL/kg/h. To reach that, “it is generally necessary to prescribe in the range of 25–30 mL/kg/h, and to minimize interruptions.” The default 25 is a starting prescribed dose — the lower end of that usual prescription range — not a delivered-dose target.

Citrate dose

Default target 3 mmol per litre of blood (about 3–4 in published examples). ACD-A is 113 mmol/L and 4% trisodium citrate is 136 mmol/L, from Oudemans-van Straaten & Ostermann, Crit Care 2012, Table 1. This is not a complete RCA protocol — calcium replacement is not calculated here.
